The FigCraft image layers also contain source links in their developer resources. The photographs are embedded in the design, including its lightbox frames.

Fraunces is the display typeface. `Fraunces.ttf` is included for reopening the design if the font is unavailable. Fine grain is sourced from the reference site's `/art/grain.png` asset.

`Pixel Hair Studio - original.figcraft` preserves the earlier pixel-art version. The main `.figcraft` file contains the revised editorial design, service-selection booking preview, and photograph lightboxes. Appointments in the prototype are sample states only.
